# Results

Small finalized research summaries and checksummed manifests may be versioned here by
experiment ID. Raw, temporary, or large output is excluded by `.gitignore`. Finalized
result files are immutable under
[Project Governance](../docs/PROJECT_GOVERNANCE.md). Frozen results currently exist for
EXP-009, EXP-012, EXP-013, and EXP-014; derived analysis artifacts are checksummed
separately from each experiment's raw manifest.
