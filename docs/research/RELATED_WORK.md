## Review protocol to define

Before a paper claim of novelty, record search databases, query strings, dates, inclusion/exclusion criteria, screening process, and a structured evidence table. Distinguish peer-reviewed work, preprints, standards, and vendor documentation. Record negative and conflicting evidence. Do not cite a source that has only been seen through a secondary summary.
